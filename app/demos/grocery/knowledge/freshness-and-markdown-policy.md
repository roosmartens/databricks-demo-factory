# Freshness, markdown and store transfer policy

Store operations policy for perishables. Fictional, for the demo.

## Markdown ladder for fresh produce
Fresh produce is marked down by 30% on the day before expiry and by 50% on the day of expiry from 15:00. Anything unsold at closing on the day of expiry goes to the food bank partner, Voedselbank, if it is safe to eat, and is otherwise recorded as waste.

## Markdown ladder for bakery
Bakery products are marked down by 50% from 17:00 on the day of baking when more than 20% of the morning bake is still on the shelf. Unsold bread goes to Voedselbank at closing.

## Markdown ladder for meat
Fresh meat is marked down by 35% two days before expiry and by 50% on the day before expiry. Meat must never be transferred between stores within two days of expiry, so use markdown instead.

## Markdown ladder for dairy
Dairy is marked down by 25% on the day before expiry.

## Store transfers
Stores can transfer perishables to each other on the 06:00 chilled van run, which links all four Randstad and Brabant stores through the Tilburg distribution centre. A transfer needs at least 20 units, must be requested in the store app before 22:00 the evening before, and the product needs at least two days of shelf life left on arrival for produce, one day for dairy. Transfers are always cheaper than waste: the internal cost is about 0.15 euro per unit.
