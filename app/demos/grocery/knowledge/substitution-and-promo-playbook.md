# Substitution and promotion playbook

Guidance for category managers and store managers. Fictional, for the demo.

## Promotions under supply risk
When confirmed supply is below 80% of the promotion forecast, the category manager decides between three options: cap the promotion per customer (for example "maximum 2 per customer"), limit the promotion to stores with enough stock, or switch the hero product. Marketing needs 48 hours' notice to change in-store signs and the app. An empty shelf during a promotion costs more trust than a cancelled promotion.

## Substitutes for strawberries
If strawberries run short, move raspberries and blueberries to the strawberry display and apply the same 2+1 mechanic where margin allows. Customers buying strawberries for desserts accept raspberries and blueberries well; mixed berry packs are a second option.

## Substitutes for avocados
Offer firm avocados with a "ripen at home" shelf label next to the ready-to-eat space. Guacamole kits are a secondary substitute.

## Substitutes for whole milk
Semi-skimmed milk and the private-label organic whole milk are accepted substitutes. Keep at least one facing of whole milk filled from the back room before opening.

## Substitutes for dairy, bakery and meat in general
Use the same category's private-label alternative first, then the closest premium alternative.
