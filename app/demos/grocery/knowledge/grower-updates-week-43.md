# Grower and supply updates, week 43

Weekly notes the fresh buying team collects from growers and importers. Fictional, for the demo.

## Zeeland Berry Growers: rain damage on autumn strawberries
Heavy rainfall in Zeeland over the weekend has damaged open-field autumn strawberries. The grower expects 25 to 35% lower yield for the next ten days and is confirming about 70% of ordered volumes. Fruit quality of what is picked is good, but berries are softer, so the grower advises a shelf life of three days instead of four. Glasshouse volumes are not affected and recover first.

## Westland Kas Collectief: tomatoes and lettuce stable
Glasshouse production in the Westland is stable. LED-lit tomato production is at plan, and iceberg lettuce volumes are normal. No supply risk expected for the next two weeks.

## Tropic Fresh Import: avocado ripening delay
Two reefer containers of avocados are held at the Port of Rotterdam for inspection. Ready-to-eat avocados will be short for about five days, and confirmed volumes for stores in South Holland are at roughly 70%. Bananas are not affected.

## Season outlook
The Dutch strawberry season ends in about two weeks. From week 45 the chain switches to imported strawberries from Huelva and Morocco, with longer lead times and higher cost.
