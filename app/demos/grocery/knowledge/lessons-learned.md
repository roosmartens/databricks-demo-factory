# Lessons learned from past incidents

Post-incident notes from store and category teams. Fictional, for the demo.

## Week 31: strawberry promotion sold out in Amsterdam
During the summer 2+1 strawberry promotion, Amsterdam Centrum sold out by Saturday 11:00 while Utrecht marked down 60 punnets on Sunday. The estimated lost sales were about 4,200 euro across the weekend. The root cause was that the promotion forecast and supplier confirmations were in different systems and nobody compared them before Thursday. The agreed fix was to check confirmed supply against the promotion forecast 72 hours before every fresh promotion, and to use store transfers before ordering from alternative suppliers.

## Week 38: sourdough waste in Rotterdam
Rotterdam Kop van Zuid kept a summer standing order for sourdough after the tourist season ended, and wasted around 25% of the bake for three weeks. Reviewing standing orders every four weeks against sell-through was added to the store manager checklist.

## Week 40: missed dairy cut-off in Eindhoven
Eindhoven Strijp missed the 14:00 dairy cut-off during a staff shortage and ran out of whole milk by 15:00 the next day. An expedited drop requested before 10:00 would have prevented it.
