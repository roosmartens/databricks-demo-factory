# Supplier agreements (summary for store operations)

Fictional summaries for the Groenhof Markets demo. Commercial terms are illustrative.

## Zeeland Berry Growers (strawberries)
Zeeland Berry Growers supplies all Dutch-grown strawberries from April to late October. Standard lead time is one day for orders placed before 11:00. The agreement has a weather and harvest clause: when yield drops because of weather, the grower may confirm less than ordered without penalty, and must tell us at least 24 hours before dispatch. When confirmed volume is below 80% of the order, buyers may source the gap from the approved alternative, Huelva Berry Cooperative, without breaching exclusivity.

## Huelva Berry Cooperative (alternative strawberries)
Huelva Berry Cooperative is the approved alternative for strawberries. Lead time is two days, delivered into the stores through the Tilburg distribution centre, with a minimum order of 20 cases (one case is 10 punnets of 400g) across all stores. Cost is about 18% higher than Zeeland Berry Growers because of transport. Shelf life on arrival is one day shorter, so order it for the days the gap actually occurs.

## Noorderland Zuivel (dairy)
Noorderland Zuivel delivers milk and yoghurt daily. The order cut-off is 14:00 for next-morning delivery. A missed cut-off can be rescued with an expedited same-day drop if requested before 10:00; the expedite fee is 85 euro per store drop. Expedites are capped at two per store per month.

## Bakkerij De Molen (bakery)
Bakkerij De Molen bakes sourdough and speciality bread to a standing order per store and weekday. The standing order can be changed until 12:00 the previous day. Unsold bread is not returnable. The bakery recommends reviewing the standing order every four weeks against actual sell-through.

## Tropic Fresh Import (bananas, avocados)
Tropic Fresh Import ripens bananas and avocados in Barendrecht. Lead time is two days. During port congestion at the Port of Rotterdam, confirmed volumes can drop by 20 to 30% for up to a week. Expedited deliveries are not possible during congestion, but ready-to-eat avocados can be swapped for firm avocados at the same price.

## Brabant Poultry (chicken)
Brabant Poultry delivers fresh chicken every two days with a two-day lead time. Orders can be reduced without a fee until 48 hours before delivery.
