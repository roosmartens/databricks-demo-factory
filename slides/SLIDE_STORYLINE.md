# Six-slide executive story
1. Opportunity: business pain, owner, decision and stakes
2. Solution: recommendation, why now, key assumptions
3. Architecture: data sources -> quality/governance -> model/logic -> users
4. Product demo: 1-2 user journeys, not a walkthrough of code
5. Business case: baseline, measured evidence, assumptions and sensitivity
6. Pilot decision: scope, KPIs, risks, owners and next step

Slide rule: one strong takeaway per slide. Avoid invented customer savings.
