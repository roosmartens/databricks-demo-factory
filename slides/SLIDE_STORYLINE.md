# Customer story: Grocery Inventory Copilot

The deck is at `/story` in the Flask app (5 slides, 16:9, press `E` to edit text live and "Download HTML" to save an offline copy). What to say on each slide, the demo script, the 30-day plan and objection handling are in `SPEAKER_NOTES.md`.

## Story arc (5 slides)

| Slide | Purpose | On the slide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · The problem and discovery | Validate the assumptions with the business leader, CDO and engineering | "Shortage in Amsterdam. Surplus in Utrecht. Same fresh product, same week." with fresh category icons (fruit, veg, fish, bakery) Three numbers, labelled as assumptions to check. The discovery questions live in the speaker notes, not on the slide |
| 2 · The art of the possible | One of their problems, solved end to end, then the live demo | Who it's for (fresh planner works in the app, store manager gets the hand-over, planning manager sees the week and asks Genie), the strawberry morning in three moves, "Open the live copilot" |
| 3 · Behind the solution | Explain the four connected layers after they have seen them work | Sources → trusted data → predict → explain → act, under one set of rules |
| 4 · Future state at scale | What the platform looks like once the pilot has proven itself | More sources (ERP, weather, loyalty), near real-time; bring it in → one trusted copy → predict and explain → act; planners, store managers, managers, growers; next use cases on the same data; one set of rules underneath |
| 5 · Next steps | Propose the 30-day pilot and get commitment | "Prove it in 30 days: one region, fresh produce, your own data." Connect (week 1), run alongside in 10 stores vs 10 similar stores (weeks 2–4), look back on last quarter in parallel (week 2), decide (day 30); what you'll know (€ on last quarter, % of recommendations accepted, same-morning decisions; ~€4M a year per point of waste at full scale); to start next week |

Discovery happens before slide 1 is explained, without slides. Every number is labelled as an assumption or as synthetic.
