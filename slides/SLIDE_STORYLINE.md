# Customer story: Grocery Inventory Copilot

The deck is at `/story` in the Flask app (5 slides, 16:9, press `E` to edit text live and "Download HTML" to save an offline copy). What to say on each slide, the demo script, the 30-day plan and objection handling are in `SPEAKER_NOTES.md`.

## Story arc (5 slides)

| Slide | Purpose | On the slide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · The problem and discovery | Validate the assumptions with the business leader, CDO and engineering | "Empty shelves in Amsterdam. Full bins in Utrecht. Same week." Three numbers, each with the question that tests it and who to ask |
| 2 · The art of the possible | One of their problems, solved end to end, then the live demo | Who uses it (fresh planner, store manager, planning manager), the strawberry morning in three moves, "Open the live copilot" |
| 3 · Behind the solution | Explain the four connected layers after they have seen them work | Sources → governed data → predict → explain → act, under Unity Catalog |
| 4 · Business value and future state | How Databricks turns the prototype into a trusted platform for the whole company | €4M per point of fresh waste and three KPIs; six next use cases on the same data; 30 days → 1 quarter → 1 year |
| 5 · Next steps | Propose the 30-day pilot and get commitment | Connect, baseline, shadow, decide, plus what we need from them today |

Discovery happens before slide 1 is explained, without slides. Every number is labelled as an assumption or as synthetic.
